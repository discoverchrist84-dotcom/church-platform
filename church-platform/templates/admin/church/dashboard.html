How adding, editing and deleting work

  1. Add: open a section, find the item and press + Add. Fill in the form and press Save.
  2. Edit: press Change (or click the item’s name), update it and press Save.
  3. Delete: open the item and press the red Delete button. You always get a confirmation page that lists anything else that would be removed.
  4. Tip: to take something off the website without losing it, untick Active / Published instead of deleting.
